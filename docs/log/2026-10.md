# imagegen log: 2026-10

The dated journal of this repo, moved out of `CLAUDE.md` (which keeps the rules and one State block). One
heading per day, oldest first. Release detail is on the GitHub release notes.

## 2026-10-02

- **Battlemaps** arrived as a kind, `edit-image` only (owner request). **Nothing moves on a battlemap** (owner
  rule): the source padded to the API aspect and mapped back onto its own grid, then the drift check,
  calibrated that day on 24 live renders across 8 maps.
- **Maps ship as WebP** (the owner's call): a restyled map is 20 to 70 MB as PNG and about 4 MB as WebP; a map
  past WebP's 16383 px limit falls back to PNG.
- Released as v1.2.0 (battlemap restyles, the drift guard, WebP maps).

## 2026-10-11 to 2026-10-14

  drawn from words by default.

## 2026-10-15

- The chroma cut keys on the plate's own colour (the median of its border) instead of the named key, so a
  render that comes back off-key cuts clean: one of 18 mannequin tokens came back on (234, 32, 232) for a
  #FF00FF ask and kept a full-frame haze at alpha 1-39. The cut also reports `hazePct` (faint matte more
  than 4 px from the subject, 63% on that plate, 0 on clean ones) and `plateColor`; over 1% fails the cut
  and `auto` falls back to rembg. The plate is the regression fixture (`src/fixtures/`).
- The mannequin token library was captured (312 grey HeroForge files, six bodies in both sexes, 26 setups
  across combat, stand, twohand, dead and prone; first in the private `fvtt-assets-actors`, moved here to
  `mannequins/` the next day on the owner's ruling that they carry no copyright) and tested on
  about 120 renders. `illustration-builder` carries the route as the default for every humanoid token. The
  record is `notes/mannequin-tokens/PLAN.md`.

## 2026-10-16

- The first real mannequin run (the four PCs, about 50 renders, in another session) overturned "no
  references": a head-to-waist crop of the approved portrait as `character` holds a known face where words
  drifted. The mannequin is a guide, not a rig (the exact-pose line bought nothing). A batch of tokens spreads
  across the poses that fit the intent (owner rule). The head angle is still open.
- The mannequin library moved into this repo (`mannequins/`, `scripts/mannequin.mjs`; owner ruling: the
  grey captures carry no copyright). The token review of the first real run put the head angle down to
  two causes seen in the pixels, the captures' own bowed heads and a frontal portrait crop bound "keep
  this exact face", and the server changed: the token framing describes the head (level, looking ahead
  along the ground, brow and eyes showing) instead of "tilts up toward the viewer"; a `character`
  reference binds features, hair, skin, build and kit only; a new `object` role reproduces a gear crop;
  `edit-image` kind `token` takes `source: "mannequin"` and writes the guide, head and no-grey line
  itself; both render tools take `thinking` (Nano Banana 2.1: minimal, medium, high) and report
  `thinkingTokens`, billed at the text rate and folded into the estimate. Next: per-PC token-head
  anchors, gear crops, and a twelve-render head test once the server is restarted.
- The head test, after the restart (fourteen renders): an absolute head line left every head bowed
  like the capture; a relative sentence lifting the head from the bowed position to a stated target
  landed four of four, on the bowed captures and on the owner's raised-head re-exports alike, so it
  now leads the tool's mannequin prompt. Gear crops as `object` references came through clean. Three
  raised-head captures are filed over their old ones. Token-head anchors from the portrait were tried
  first and are not needed; a frontal token head as a `pose` reference pulls the face frontal.
- Round two: `thinking: "high"` bought nothing on tokens (same counts, one stance drifted), so it
  stays unset; the lifting sentence held on combat and two-handed, read as alert on prone and did
  no harm on dead, so the tool carries it on every mannequin edit; after a restart, one render with
  nothing about the head in the instruction confirmed the tool's own line lifts it. Three raised-head
  captures replaced their old ones; a fourth, filed as a one-body `combat-dagger-open` setup, was
  dropped the same day as redundant with `dagger-shield` minus the shield. The library is not
  re-exported: the raised heads added nothing the lifting sentence had not already done.
- The skill is campaign-neutral (owner rule): every finish word moved to the campaign shelf, which now
  carries an icon line too; the skill keeps the proven prompt shapes with `<the shelf's ... finish>`
  placeholders, and its lessons name roles (a key NPC, a mine map), never a campaign, a PC or a scene.
  The server, its tests and the backlog use neutral example labels.
- The head check: a mannequin token's plate goes to a judge model (`gemini-3.5-flash-lite`,
  `src/head.ts`) for one word, a bowed or up first render is redone once, and the result reports
  `head`; two bad verdicts deliver flagged, a judge outage never blocks. The words-only token
  framing was confirmed on a wolf (seen along its back); a beholder came out more frontal than
  top-down, a corner the upright wording does not cover. Per-character HeroForge builds are ruled
  out (copyright). The four PCs' condition sets (stand, combat, prone, dead) were rendered from
  the live tool line and staged for the owner.
