## 2026-10-15

- The chroma cut keys on the plate's own colour (the median of its border) instead of the named key, so a
  render that comes back off-key cuts clean: one of 18 mannequin tokens came back on (234, 32, 232) for a
  #FF00FF ask and kept a full-frame haze at alpha 1-39. The cut also reports `hazePct` (faint matte more
  than 4 px from the subject, 63% on that plate, 0 on clean ones) and `plateColor`; over 1% fails the cut
  and `auto` falls back to rembg. The plate is the regression fixture (`src/fixtures/`).
- The mannequin token library was captured (312 grey HeroForge files, six bodies in both sexes, 26 setups
  across combat, stand, twohand, dead and prone; first in the private `fvtt-assets-actors`, moved here to
  `mannequins/` the next day on the owner's ruling that they carry no copyright) and tested on
  about 120 renders. `illustration-builder` carries the route as the default for every humanoid token. The
  record is `notes/mannequin-tokens/PLAN.md`.
