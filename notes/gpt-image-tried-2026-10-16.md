# GPT Image tried, 2026-10-16: no

**The owner's verdict: too photoreal.** OpenAI's GPT Image 2.5 Flare (`gpt-image-2.5-flare`, quality
`high`) was tried against this server's Gemini flash in two rounds, about $1.10 of GPT renders. Its
look is granular, gritty, dark and close to photographic, nowhere near the shelf's semi-realistic
smooth blended shading, and a style reference only moved it partway. Gemini stays the one provider;
the scope rule stands. The spike branch was deleted; this note is the record.

What it showed, for whoever asks again:

- **Native transparency is real.** `background: "transparent"` with PNG out gives a true alpha
  channel, cleaner than the chroma cut on smoke, steam, fur and hair (the figure sits at alpha
  252 to 253, a faint 1 to 3 haze on the empty ground; both clamp in one line). The one thing it
  did better.
- **Words-only tokens failed the camera**: a high three-quarter view, chest and boots showing, a
  wolf walking toward the bottom of the frame.
- **On the mannequin route the camera held.** The four Elders through their own pipeline (a
  portrait from the shelf template, a head-to-waist crop, then the capture plus the crop as one
  edit) came back overhead, with likeness and kit carried, and the head judge called all four
  `right`. The finish was still the problem.
- **Prices.** About 5 to 7.5 cents a 1024 token, 6.6 cents a 2560x1600 illustration (any size in
  multiples of 16, so no crop), 15 cents a map restyle; token-billed at $8 per million image
  input tokens, $30 per million output.
- **One battlemap restyle** passed the drift check but turned the Great Hall's stone pillars into
  lit braziers, a content change the drift check cannot see.
